# Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background, experience, age,
disability, nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender identity or expression, or
sexual orientation. Treat contributors, users, and maintainers with respect.

Use considerate language, ask questions in good faith, give constructive feedback,
and accept corrections. Critique ideas and code, not people. Respect privacy,
access needs, and different levels of technical experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information without permission. Do not retaliate against someone
who raises a concern or participates in a review.

These standards apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces,
and when someone represents this project in public.

## Reporting a concern

For abusive content on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
relevant content or profile. See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub handles those reports under its own policies; this is not a private inbox
for this project's maintainer.

For project moderation, contact the repository maintainer through an existing
private channel if you already have one. Otherwise, you may open an issue asking
only for a private contact route. Do not include the incident, personal details,
or identifying evidence in that public request. Wait for a private route before
sharing sensitive details. This repository does not currently designate a
separate confidential conduct-reporting address.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, warn participants, limit
participation, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants from project
spaces, according to the severity and pattern of behavior. They should explain
the reason when practical, consider relevant context, and protect reporters'
privacy. Reports involving a maintainer may instead be directed to GitHub through
the reporting route above.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to stop. A person seeking
review of a moderation decision may request reconsideration respectfully through
the same private channel, without repeating or escalating the conduct.
